# Throne of Doom and Slaughter

*Known in the ranks as the Knight of Judgment and Inquisition — a pure offensive throne, matched more closely to the Retribution Path than any other. Its knights don't debate a sentence. They deliver it, blade first, and let the verdict speak for itself.*

> **Prerequisite: Retribution Path.** You must have chosen the Retribution Path at 1st level to select this Sacred Throne.

## Designer's Note

An earlier version of this Throne's capstone granted flat bonus damage per hit instead of Fervor. Combined with Blood for Blood and Unrelenting Carnage — both of which increase how many attacks you make in a turn — that version pushed damage per round roughly 150% above the other Sacred Thrones' baseline, with no natural ceiling. Final Verdict's current form (Fervor returned per kill, rather than damage per hit) keeps the "kill and keep swinging" payoff this Throne is built around, while being naturally bounded by how many enemies you can actually finish off in a turn instead of compounding endlessly with attack count.

## Sacred Throne Features

### 3rd Level: Blood for Blood

*A death met in battle pays its own price back to the one who dealt it.*

Whenever an attack made with Cross Slash reduces a target to 0 hit points, you immediately regain Fervor and hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.

### 6th Level: Sealed Fate

*Every second cut opens a third wound.*

Cross Slash's cost for you is always calculated as though you'd already taken its Improve Fervor Technique upgrade (N+1), starting at 3rd level. If you later select Cross Slash's own improvement via Improve Fervor Technique, its cost drops further, but no lower than N (never below its improved rate).

### 10th Level: Unrelenting Carnage

*No ward built of flesh and bone stops this judgment.*

Attacks made with Cross Slash gain 5 feet of Speed each time you attack and your movement don't provoke opportunity attacks until the end of your turn.

### 14th Level: Final Verdict

*The end of one sentence is the beginning of the next.*

For every Cross Slash attack you make this turn, you gain 1 **Verdict** stack and it unleash when you make last Cross Slash attack. You roll an additional attack roll of 1 and deal additional damage equal to your weapon damage die times the number of Verdict you have. you can use this once per short rest. 

### 17th Level: Throne of Doom and Slaughter

*Judgment, once begun, does not slow down.*

You can activate this Throne by spending spell slots with a combined level of 10 (calculated the same way as converting Fervor, but spent to activate the Throne rather than banked as Fervor). Once activated, the Throne's effect lasts for 1 minute.

While active, each time you attack, your next attack that turn deals 2 additional damage until the end of your turn, and you gain 5 feet of Speed each time you attack.
