# Novice

*Every legend starts at zero. A Novice is an adventurer who hasn't committed to a trade yet — someone who spends their first couple of fights learning to hit things, patch themselves up, and survive long enough to matter. That changes fast: starting at 3rd level, a Novice undergoes the first of four Job Changes, each one a genuine shift in identity rather than a trickle of new numbers, climbing from Second Class all the way to Fourth Class by the time they reach the top of their profession.*

## Designer's Note

The Novice is built around the class-change ladder from *Ragnarok Online* (and its modern mobile version, *Ragnarok X: Next Generation*): Novice → First Class → Second Class → Transcendent Second Class → Third Class → Fourth Class. A standard D&D 2024 subclass asks you to commit once, early, and that's it — RO's job system is built around re-committing again and again, at real narrative turning points. This class keeps that feeling by skipping First Class as a separate mechanical step (you choose a Job Path that starts directly at the Second Class tier at 3rd level) and by placing four true "job change" milestones at 3rd, 7th, 11th, and 15th level — chosen specifically so none of them land on the same level as an Ability Score Improvement.

## Mana

A Novice's Job Skills — Strike, First Aid, Play Dead, and every technique granted by a Job Path — are powered by a pool of **Mana**.

**Mana Pool.** Your pool equals your Proficiency Bonus (2 at 1st level, rising to 6 at 17th level and beyond — see the Mana Pool column on the Novice table). You regain all spent Mana when you finish a Long Rest.

**Recovering Mana Mid-Day.** There's no Short Rest recovery. Outside of a Long Rest, the only way to recover Mana is Play Dead (see *2nd Level: Play Dead*), which you can use twice per day.

**Spending Mana.** Every Job Skill lists its own Mana cost. If you don't have enough Mana left to pay for one, you can't use it — but Strike never costs more than 1 Mana and is always available, so you're never left with nothing to do.

**Saving Throw DC.** Any Job Skill that calls for a saving throw uses DC = 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + your Primary Ability modifier.

## Extra Attack

Starting at 5th level, when you take the Attack action, you can attack **twice** instead of once. Each of those attacks can independently be a normal weapon attack or the use of a Job Skill whose casting time is an action, mixed freely in any combination — the only restriction is that **you can't use the same named Job Skill more than once in the same turn.**

Starting at 20th level (*Extra Attack Improvement*), this becomes **three** attacks per Attack action, under the same restriction.

## Class Features

As a Novice, you gain the following class features.

### 1st Level: Strike

You gain the Strike Job Skill (see *Base Job Skills*, below) — a reliable weapon attack that never costs more than 1 Mana and is always available, even with an empty Mana pool.

### 1st Level: First Aid

You gain the First Aid Job Skill (see *Base Job Skills*, below), letting you patch up yourself or an ally within melee range.

### 2nd Level: Play Dead

You gain the Play Dead Job Skill (see *Base Job Skills*, below) — your only way to recover Mana outside of a Long Rest.

### 3rd Level: Job Change — Second Class

You undergo your first Job Change: choose a **Job Path** (see *Job Paths*, below). Your Job Path sets your Primary Ability, grants additional proficiencies, and opens up a pool of **Job Path Job Skills** unique to that path.

Choose **2** Job Path Job Skills from your Job Path's pool. Each begins at **Rank 1** (see *Job Skills*, below).

### 4th Level: Ability Score Improvement

As normal. You gain this feature again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

### 5th Level: Extra Attack

See *Extra Attack*, above.

### 6th Level: Super Novice

Choose **1** additional Job Path Job Skill from your Job Path's pool, at Rank 1.

### 7th Level: Job Change — Transcendent Second Class

You undergo your second Job Change. See your Job Path's document for the feature this grants.

### 8th Level: Ability Score Improvement

### 9th Level: Job Skill Advancement

Every Job Skill you know — your 3 base Job Skills (Strike, First Aid, Play Dead) and every Job Path Job Skill you've chosen so far — advances to **Rank 2** at the same time. This isn't a choice; every Job Skill you know goes up together, and none is left behind.

### 10th Level: Ultra Super Novice

You add half your Proficiency Bonus (round down) to any ability check you make that doesn't already include your Proficiency Bonus.

### 11th Level: Job Change — Third Class

You undergo your third Job Change. See your Job Path's document for the feature this grants.

### 12th Level: Ability Score Improvement

### 13th Level: Job Skill Advancement

As 9th level: every Job Skill you know advances to **Rank 3**.

### 14th Level: Elevated Novice

Choose one ability score you don't already have saving throw proficiency in. You gain proficiency in saving throws using that ability score.

### 15th Level: Job Change — Fourth Class

You undergo your fourth and final Job Change, reaching the top of your profession. See your Job Path's document for the feature this grants.

### 16th Level: Ability Score Improvement

### 17th Level: Job Skill Advancement

As 9th level: every Job Skill you know advances to **Rank 4**, its maximum.

### 18th Level: Hyper Novice

Once per Long Rest, when you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead.

### 19th Level: Ability Score Improvement

### 20th Level: Extra Attack Improvement

See *Extra Attack*, above — you can now make 3 attacks with the Attack action instead of 2.

## Job Skills

Every Job Skill you know has a **Rank** from 1 to 4 that determines how strong it is. You never spend points or make choices to rank a Job Skill up — every Job Skill you know advances together, automatically, at 9th, 13th, and 17th level (see *Job Skill Advancement*, above).

**Base Job Skills (3, automatic).** You gain all 3 base Job Skills without choosing — Strike and First Aid at 1st level, Play Dead at 2nd level. See *Base Job Skills*, below, for their full rules text.

**Job Path Job Skills (3 total).** You choose 2 at 3rd level and 1 more at 6th level, all from your Job Path's Job Skill pool.

Because Job Skill Advancement happens at 9th, 13th, and 17th level, all 6 Job Skills you know (3 base + 3 Job Path) reach **Rank 4** together at 17th level — none of them ever falls behind.

**No Respec.** Once you choose a Job Path Job Skill at 3rd or 6th level, it's yours for the rest of the game.

## Base Job Skills

The 3 Job Skills below are the only ones that exist so far — every Job Path grants its own additional Job Skills once that path is written (see *Job Paths*, below).

### Strike
*Job Skill*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 5 feet
**Mana Cost:** 1

You make one weapon attack against a creature within range, with a **+2** bonus to the attack roll.

*Job Skill Advancement.* The bonus increases to +3 at 9th level, +4 at 13th level, and +5 at 17th level.

### First Aid
*Job Skill*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Mana Cost:** 1

You touch a creature within melee range — yourself or an ally — and channel restorative energy into it. The target regains **2d8** hit points.

*Job Skill Advancement.* The healing and Mana cost both increase: 4d8 (2 Mana) at 9th level, 6d8 (3 Mana) at 13th level, and 8d8 (4 Mana) at 17th level.

### Play Dead
*Job Skill*

**Casting Time:** Action (takes 1 minute to complete, and only works outside combat)
**Range:** Self
**Mana Cost:** 0

You lie down and feign death for 1 minute. At the end, you regain **all** of your spent Mana. You can use this feature **twice**, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

*Job Skill Advancement.* None — Play Dead doesn't change with Rank.
