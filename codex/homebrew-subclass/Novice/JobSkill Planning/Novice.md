# Novice — Job Skills (First Aid & Play Dead)

> Extracted from [Novice-Base.md](../Concept/Novice-Base.md) — only the **First Aid** and **Play Dead** tracks, formatted as spell-card-style Job Skills. Both are gained automatically (no pick required) and auto-upgrade in Rank together with every other Track the character holds, at level 9, 13, and 17.

---

### STRIKE
*Job Skill*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** 5 feet
**Mana Cost:** 1
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You make one weapon attack against a creature within range with **+2** attack rolls. 

***Job Skill Upgrade.*** The attack rolls bonus increases by 1 at levels 9 (**+3**), 13 (**+4**), and 17 (**+5**).

---

### FIRST AID
*Job Skill*

**Casting Time:** Action
**Range:** Touch
**Mana Cost:** 1
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature within melee range — yourself or an ally — and channel restorative energy into it. The target regains **2d8** hit points.

***Job Skill Upgrade.*** The healing increases by 2d8, and the mana cost increases by 1, when you reach levels 9 (**4d8**, 2 mana), 13 (**6d8**, 3 mana), and 17 (**8d8**, 4 mana).

---

### PLAY DEAD
*Job Skill*

**Range:** Self
**Mana Cost:** 0
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You lie down and feign death for 10 minute. At the end, you regain **all** of your expended mana. You can use this feature **twice**, and regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

***Job Skill Upgrade.*** None — this Job Skill doesn't change with Rank.
